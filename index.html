SOVEREIGN PRIVACY ARCHITECTURE

NUTRO Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 • Ecosystem Versions: NUTRO Normal v1.0.0 & NUTRO+ AI v2.1.0

1. Fundamental Principle: Zero Telemetry & Data Sovereignty

The NUTRO ecosystem (including NUTRO Normal Edition and NUTRO+ AI Special Edition) is founded on the conviction that your nutritional intake, body measurements, menstrual cycles, and biometric goals are strictly intimate personal data.

We do not embed third-party advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, behavioral trackers, or analytics collectors. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or harvest user information.

2. On-Device SQLite Storage

In both editions, all meal logs, nutritional summaries, water intake counters, and menstrual cycle calendars are written to an encrypted, on-device SQLite database stored exclusively in your Android sandbox directory. If you delete the app or clear app data, the records are wiped from your device.

3. Device Permissions & Hardware Access

  • Camera: Used strictly in real-time when you trigger the ML Kit Barcode Scanner or the Gemini AI Vision Plate Scanner. Frames are processed in memory and are never persisted to disk or sent to advertisement networks.
  • Microphone (NUTRO+ Only): Used strictly when you tap the Sree AI voice companion button to transcribe spoken requests for nutritional queries. Audio data is not recorded or stored.
  • Network Access: NUTRO Normal is completely offline. NUTRO+ AI uses network access solely for real-time Gemini AI vision and voice inference.

4. Zero Telemetry & Independent Privacy

In NUTRO+ AI Special Edition, AI requests are transmitted securely and ephemerally for instant nutritional calculation. No user accounts, tracking profiles, or telemetry streams are ever recorded, stored, or monetized.
